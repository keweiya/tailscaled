Routed prefixes

Destinations listed here are sent into the tunnel. One prefix per line, # starts a comment. Add a peer's advertised subnet (for example 192.168.100.0/24) to reach its LAN from any browser.

Remember that tailscale set --accept-routes must also be on, otherwise tailscaled drops the packets it receives for those subnets.
